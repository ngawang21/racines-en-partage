## Data collected and purposes

We only collect the data needed for your order: name, e-mail address, phone number, billing address, delivery address or chosen pickup point, and the content of the order. It is used to pack and deliver your order, send you the confirmation and answer your questions. Legal basis: performance of the sales contract and our accounting obligations.

## Service providers

Your data is only shared with the following providers, for the needs of the order:

- Stripe (online payment and receipts);
- Cloudflare (website hosting and technical processing of requests);
- Resend (sending the order confirmation e-mails);
- Mondial Relay (pickup point delivery: name, phone and e-mail for the collection notice);
- La Poste / Colissimo (home delivery: name, address and phone);
- GitHub (storage of the website content, used only by the administrator; no customer data is stored there).

Some of these providers are based in the United States; transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Basket and cookies

Your basket is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage), on your device only: it is not sent to us before you order. The website uses no advertising cookie and no audience measurement tool that tracks you, so no consent banner is needed.

## Retention periods

Order data is kept for 10 years to meet accounting obligations. E-mail exchanges are kept for 3 years after the last contact.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, write to {email}. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).
